01 — Our Story

A studio built on joy.

Perfectly Planned began with a simple conviction — that planning a wedding should feel as joyful as the day itself.

For more than a decade, our family-owned studio has guided couples across Texas through every season of the planning journey. We are detail people and feelings people in equal measure: we sweat the timeline and the floor plan so that you can stay fully present for the moments that matter.

We believe a wedding should be unmistakably yours — never a template, never a trend borrowed from someone else's day. Every couple we serve gets a plan shaped around their love story, their people, and their budget — with a calm, steady presence from the very first inquiry to the final send-off.

That is the heart of what we do: joyful celebrations of love, and a simple, stress-free path to get there.

i

We listen before we plan

ii

On the day itself, you should be fully present. We hold the timeline, the vendors, and every quiet contingency.
