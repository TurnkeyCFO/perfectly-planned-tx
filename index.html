01 — The Studio

A boutique studio for the genuinely in love.

We create joyful celebrations of love — and a calm, simple, stress-free path to get there.

Perfectly Planned is a family-owned wedding planning & coordination studio in the heart of Bryan–College Station. For more than a decade we have walked beside couples across Texas — listening first, designing carefully, and quietly carrying the details so you never have to.

A wedding should feel unmistakably like the two of you — never a template. Every timeline, floor plan, and conversation is shaped around your love story and your budget.

  • Budget-honest design
