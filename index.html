01 — The Studio

A boutique studio for the
genuinely in love.

Perfectly Planned is a family-owned wedding planning & coordination studio in the heart of Bryan–College Station. For more than a decade we have walked alongside couples across Texas — listening first, designing carefully, and carrying the weight of the details so you never have to.

We believe a wedding should feel unmistakably like the two of you — not a template, not a trend. Every timeline, floor plan, and conversation is built around your love story and your budget, with a calm, joyful presence from the first inquiry to the last dance.
